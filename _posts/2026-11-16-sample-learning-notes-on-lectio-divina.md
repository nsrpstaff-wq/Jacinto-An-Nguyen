This is a sample learning note. Use this section for shorter pieces: what you read this week, a summary of a lecture, or a concept you want to explain clearly.

## A simple structure that works

1. **What I read or studied:** the source, with a short citation.
2. **The main ideas:** three to five points in your own words.
3. **What I'm taking away:** a question, a connection, or how it changes your thinking.

Short is fine. The point is to keep a record of what you are learning and share it.
