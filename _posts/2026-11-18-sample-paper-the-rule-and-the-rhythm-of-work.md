## Headings organize the argument

Use `##` for main sections and `###` for subsections. Footnotes work the way you would expect in a paper.[^1] You can cite as many sources as you need.[^2]

> Quotations set off from the text look like this. They are good for longer block quotes from primary sources.

### Lists and emphasis

- *Italic* for titles and emphasis
- **Bold** for key terms
- Numbered lists work too
