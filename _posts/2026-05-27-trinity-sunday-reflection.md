In this reflection for Trinity Sunday, I reflect on how the God who acts throughout human history is the very same God who exists eternally as a communion of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit. Because the triune God is relational and open rather than closed in on himself, we who believe are called to reflect that divine life by opening ourselves to the community around us.
