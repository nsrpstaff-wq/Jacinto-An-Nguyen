This is a sample reflection. Reflections are personal essays: something you lived through, noticed, or are still turning over.

They don't need footnotes or an abstract. Write the way you would tell the story to a friend, then tighten it.

A good reflection often moves from a concrete moment, to what it meant, to what you are carrying forward.

Delete this file when you publish your first real reflection.
